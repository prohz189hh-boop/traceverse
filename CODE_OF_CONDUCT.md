# Code of conduct

TraceVerse welcomes people of every background and experience level. Contributors, maintainers, and users are expected to communicate respectfully in repository discussions, issues, pull requests, and other spaces maintained for this project.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate, patient, and clear. Review code and ideas rather than making personal judgments.
- Welcome newcomers and explain feedback in a way that helps people improve.
- Respect different perspectives and accept responsibility when your words or actions cause harm.
- Respect people's privacy. Ask before sharing personal information or private communications.

Harassment, discriminatory language, sexual attention, threats, doxxing, and sustained disruption are not acceptable. Disagreement is welcome; intimidation and personal attacks are not.

## Reporting and enforcement

For a conduct concern, contact the repository owner through an available private contact on their [GitHub profile](https://github.com/prohz189hh-boop). Do not publish private details in an issue. GitHub's reporting and blocking tools can be used when a private project contact is unavailable.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close disruptive threads, restrict participation, or ban accounts according to the severity and repetition of behavior. Reports will be handled discreetly as far as practical; do not promise confidentiality that the platform cannot provide. Maintainers are expected to apply this policy consistently and recuse themselves from cases where they have a conflict.

This policy applies to project spaces and to people representing TraceVerse publicly.
